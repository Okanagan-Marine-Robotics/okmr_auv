# Startup procedure

(super important you do it in this order)

## Step 0

Create a network profile on your main testing computer with these parameters:
- ipv4: 192.168.2.1/24 -> is the gateway DHCP server 
- internet sharing on through the wired connection via connecting to a wifi network

Make sure the profile is activated before connecting!!!

## Step 1
IF TESTING SERVROS: open canister and locate the yellow green battery plug. Plug a battery of less than 7.6 V to the connector. a green light should emit.

Plug in the batteries (2) to one or each side of the vehicle (black first, then red). 


Wait for Jetson to initialize (observable through onboard lights + maybe the bendy screen ..??)


## Step 2

Connect the tether to the main computer (directly or through a switch)

## Step 3 (optional)

At this point, you can connect other computers to the switch.
